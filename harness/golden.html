Golden-vector manifest (scaffolding — review F8)

Conformance model: decoders must be byte-exact against committed frames; encoders are conformant iff a reference decoder recovers their output. The reference rasterizer is the analytic per-pixel renderer with deterministic trig (dtrig.js) and 8-bit quantized output. This page emits the MANIFEST — profile, seed, per-frame SHA-256 of quantized frames — which pins the vectors textually until a PNG-writing runtime is available. Final vectors freeze only after Phase 0 sets real constants.
