phryctoros — the optical carriage harness

A screen shows rotating rings; a camera films them; these pages emit, receive, test, and record. The repository's README.md is the overview, and docs/ holds the architecture, the testing guide, and the field workflow.

Field work — from ticket to record (docs/field-workflow.md)

  • orders.html — the order window: the capture queue against the posted evidence; the top card is the next capture, handed to the take console with one click
  • take.html — the take console: one card arms the synthetic twin, the emitter, and the receiver with the same settings; the take id and the shot's conditions ride into the harvest log
  • emit.html — the emitter: validate, read the flicker report, Start, Fullscreen; exports the loop as a WebM video
  • receive.html — the receiver: video-file harvest, live camera harvest, synthetic twins (?synth=). It also runs from file:// for recorded clips, and dist/receive-standalone.html is the same receiver in one file
  • registry.html — the capture registry: harvest logs plus physical conditions, extracted metrics, standards, range-falloff fits, and the re-run lane

Testing (docs/testing.md)

  • test.html — suite 1: the v2 core and the harvest family
  • test-v3.html — suite 2: conic correction, the v3 emitter and decoder core, tiling
  • test-saddle.html — suite 3: saddle registration and the tilt referees
  • test-v3-dring.html — suite 4: the beacon, chunked framing, the lease, arrays
  • test-track.html — suite 5: the continuous receiver
  • runner.html — the job channel: runs suites and scripts from jobs/next.json

Keep a running suite's tab in front: browsers throttle background tabs.

Diagnostics

  • diag.html — stage-by-stage autopsy of one field clip
  • batch-diag.html — batch autopsy: one row per capture
  • elim.html — liar elimination over an exported store
  • pool36.html — the field36 pooled peel (a historical specimen)
  • selfchar.html — self-characterisation v0: latency and refresh/capture beat (untested on hardware)
  • golden.html — golden-vector manifest scaffolding
Photosensitivity: the emission is a rotating concentric pattern; local flicker near a ring edge is k·frot per harmonic. The emitter page computes the flicker report before it will start and never autoplays. Nobody needs to watch the screen for the link to work.
