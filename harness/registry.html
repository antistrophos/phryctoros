capture registry — proposed standards against the evidence

Each capture record = one harvest log (synthetic / render-export / camera) + the physical conditions the log cannot know (distance, angle, zoom, lighting, device, mount). Metrics are extracted from the log, never typed by hand: per-ring lock duty and first lock, wire rate (avg/peak), decoded goodput, exit-vs-loop, bind/seal windows. The registry itself is results/registry.json — saved through the same POST lane every instrument uses, with a download backup; commit it when it is worth keeping. Ingest from the server's own posted logs (scan), from downloaded harvest-log-*.json files, or by paste.
